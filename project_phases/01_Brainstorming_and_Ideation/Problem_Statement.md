# PocketSmart AI – Problem Statement

## Problem

People often find it difficult to plan their spending for different
needs such as home decoration, parties, events, and jewelry.

Users may have a fixed budget but may not know how to divide that
budget effectively or which options are suitable for their requirements.

## Proposed Solution

PocketSmart AI is a GenAI-powered recommendation system that provides
personalized suggestions according to the user's requirements and budget.

The system provides recommendations for:

1. Home planning
2. Party planning
3. Jewelry suggestions

## Target Users

- Students
- Families
- Event organizers
- Shoppers
- People planning home improvements

## Main Objective

The objective of PocketSmart AI is to help users make practical,
budget-aware planning decisions through an easy-to-use AI application.
